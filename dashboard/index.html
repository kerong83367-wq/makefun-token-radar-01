implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × breadth

CategoryDexs & Perpetuals 0.35, Lending & CDP 0.30, Prediction 0.28, RWA 0.25, Risk Curators 0.24, Insurance 0.22, Yield 0.20, Stablecoins 0.18, Bridge 0.15, other 0.25. A venue that takes a cut of every trade is worth more per locked dollar than a pass-through.
Growth7-day TVL change, clamped to −50% / +150%; multiplier 1 + growth × 0.6.
Maturity0.65 + 0.35 × √(min(age, 1460 days) / 1460). A protocol that ran two years with no token is steadier than a two-week-old one; age alone never doubles a value. Unknown age counts as 365 days.
Breadth1 + 0.03 per extra chain, up to 4. More chains, more surface for a token to land on.

Every factor comes from the snapshot itself — TVL, 7-day change, listing date, category, chains. No name is ever special-cased. Already-trading protocols are shown separately, at the market's own values, never mixed into the ranking.
